# Final test with a co-designer

One page for a teammate. Do the session with a Deaf co-designer. Do not help unless they ask. Write what happened in `docs/cocreation-notes.md`. Do not invent a result before the session.

## Before you start

- Date, place, and the co-designer's name, written the way they want to be credited.
- Browser, and a phone if you have one. Note the width if you can.
- Sound can be off. Captions and the sign window are the point.
- Open `http://localhost:3000/demo/index.html` after `npm start` in `sign-window/`.

## The task, said once

"Open the lesson, switch to Amharic captions, make them bigger, move the sign window, and finish the lesson without help."

## What to watch

- Can they start the video from the Play control?
- Can they find አማርኛ or Amharic and turn those captions on?
- Can they make the caption text larger (A+ or Text size) and still read it?
- Can they open Sign window and move it, or on a phone see it under the video instead of covering the controls?
- Can they reach the end of the lesson?
- Where did they stop, and what did they try just before that?

## Where to write it

`docs/cocreation-notes.md`

- Date, place, co-designer, facilitator
- The task, in the words you used
- What they finished without help
- Where they got stuck
- What they asked you to change
- What you changed afterwards

If you also use NVDA, TalkBack, or browser zoom at 200% and 400%, write those in `docs/test-log.md`. Leave the template blank until a person has done the test.
